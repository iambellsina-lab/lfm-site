No. 01  —  What is actually happening

The business grew into your life, and nobody ever agreed to it.

There was no conversation. The hours crept up, one of you picked up everything that got dropped, and the arrangement you are both living in now was never decided by either of you.

Meanwhile you are chasing a revenue target somebody picked because it sounded big. Almost nobody works out the figure that matters: what your life costs to run each month, and what you need to bring home to cover it.

You did not choose this. You drifted into it.

Bokhari, Bao, Kwon and Yu followed 628 couples from 1991 to 2022. When one partner starts a business, the other one's mental health drops measurably. For couples whose business grew, lost time together was the bigger problem. The partner takes on what the founder used to handle, in the study's own words, “often without anyone explicitly agreeing to the new arrangement.”

you do not have a money problem.
