No. 01  —  Refund policy

Refund policy

Fourteen days on The Life First Plan, Radical Responsibility and Radical Responsibility VIP. If you have started the work and it is the wrong thing for you, email us inside fourteen days of purchase and we refund it in full. You do not have to justify it.

Build the Bridge runs on coaching terms rather than a refund window. It is six months of private sessions sold by application, and we agree the terms with you in writing before you pay. If we do not think it is a fit, we say so on the call and you do not buy it.

Payment plans. If you are mid-plan and inside the refund window, we refund what you have paid and cancel the remaining payments. Outside the window the remaining payments stand.

Refunds are processed to the original payment method and usually clear within a week.

Terms

Buying a level gives one household access to the material in it. A partner's seat is included or discounted as each level's page states, and that covers the two of you rather than a wider team.

Prices rise when a level reaches a set number of members, never on a date. They do not come back down, and members keep the price they paid at the time they paid it.

Materials, workbooks, recordings and the Playbook are ours and are licensed to you for your own use. Please do not redistribute, resell or publish them.

Live calls are recorded so members who miss one can watch it back. If you would rather not appear in a recording, keep your camera off and tell us.

We can decline or end a working relationship if someone is abusive to us or to another member. In that case we refund the unused portion.

No. 03  —  Privacy

Privacy

We collect what you give us: your name, your email address, and whatever you type into the Gap Scan or a form. We use it to deliver what you bought and to write to you about it.

Payments are handled by Stripe and we never see or store your card details.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone outside the tools we use to run this: Stripe for payments, our email and CRM platform, and the community platform where members gather.

The figures you enter into the Gap Scan are yours. We use them in aggregate to understand what the scan is telling us, never attached to your name in anything published.

Ask us at any time for a copy of what we hold about you, or for it to be deleted, and we will do it.
