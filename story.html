A note from us

We ran the order backwards for years. The income came first, and the life got whatever was left over at the end of it. We were good at it, which is the part that makes it hard to notice.

What finally changed it was not another strategy. It was sitting down together and writing out the life we actually wanted, in detail, before a single dollar figure entered the room. Then pricing it. Then building the income to match.

That is the whole method, and it is the order almost nobody uses.

We coach it together, both of us in every session, because the problem shows up between two people and it gets solved between two people.

If you already did the hard part and still cannot agree on what it was for, you are in the right place.

Bella & Dalton
